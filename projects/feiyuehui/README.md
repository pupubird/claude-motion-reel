# 翡月荟 · 拨云见月 — reel 05

![Poster frame](v4/docs/poster.jpg)

A 40-second brand film for 翡月荟, a jadeite house (你的翡翠管家). The founder's line 拨开云雾见明月 is staged
literally: the imperial-jade ring rises out of a sea of cloud like the moon. Then come the craft in three acts
(设计 · 雕刻 描金 · 镶嵌), the collection orbiting the sunrise, and a gold phoenix lockup.
[Watch the release](https://github.com/pupubird/claude-motion-reel/releases/tag/feiyuehui-v1.0).

The film is rendered in code (three.js), then finished photoreal:
1. It is rendered again as grey clay.
2. Seedance 2.5 re-renders it shot by shot against the client's own product photographs, and every piece is checked
   against its photograph.
3. Every title and the logo are composited back from the code render.

Source, run instructions and the playbook are in [`v4/`](v4/). Start at [`v4/README.md`](v4/README.md), then
[`v4/LEARNING.md`](v4/LEARNING.md).

The client consented (2026-09-30) to the use of its site's images for this film. Those images stay local, and so does
everything built from them: the 3D asset package, the logo traces and the Seedance takes. The three earlier
iterations of the film (v1–v3) stay local too.

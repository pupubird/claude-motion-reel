# 翡月荟 v4 — 拨云见月 · storyboard

40 s · 120 BPM (1 beat = 30 frames, 1 bar = 2 s, 20 bars) · 1920×1080 · 60 fps · three.js, every frame code-rendered.
Anchors live in `src/config.js` (`T`); the cue sheet (`tools/cues.py`) and the score plan (`tools/music.py --variant c`)
read the same grid.

**Concept.** The founder's own line on the brand page — 拨开云雾见明月, *part the clouds and mist, see the bright
moon* — staged literally, then carried through the brand line 聚日月精华 (*gather the essence of sun and moon*): the
film opens on the moon (月) and its climax is the sunrise (日); everything gathers (聚/荟) into the name. The circle —
moon, bangle, ring, moon window, the ring of six round the sun — is the one shape every act returns to.

Hand-offs carry something across every boundary: cloud → ring (it rises out of it) → hoop (the dive) → the gold of
1% → gold lines (设计) → the moon window's light (雕刻 → 镶嵌) → the sunrise (the drop) → the light the six become →
the gold of the lockup.

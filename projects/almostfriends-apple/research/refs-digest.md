Source: the 12 studies in `../../almostfriends-v2/research/refs/` (01–12 + README). Every number below comes from those docs unless marked **[verified]** (checked against a frame I pulled) or **[correction]**.

**Lens applied:** the new film presents the product meaningfully, with Apple-level restraint and craft. ## 2. Deep notes on the four Apple references

### 01: iPhone 15 Chinese promo (live action, 42.4 s, 29.97 fps)

**Structure.** One person's day (home, stairs, rain, café, hotel, taxi, airport, cinema, plane), one feature per shot. The bookends match: it opens with a macro glide over the phone back and closes on the phone back as a landscape under clouds with the logo.
- Arc: fast intro (0–4), steady parade of features (4–30), lift into the red and plane section (31–37), peak at 37.0, then a hard drop to silence for the logo (38–39).

**Edit rhythm.**
- About 23 shots in 39 s. ASL is about **1.7 s** by eye. The scene detector counts only 11 hard cuts (ASL 3.2 s) because most transitions are whips or foreground wipes.
- Shot lengths: 0.5, 2.0, 1.5, 2.0, 0.7, 2.3, 3.0, 3.0, 1.5, 3.5, 0.7, 2.0, 2.4, 2.2, 1.9, 1.9, 1.6, 1.5, 1.6, 2.8, 1.7 s.
- Cuts land on or just before music accents, which come every 1.0–1.75 s.

**Type.**
- Feature captions in white PingFang-style **regular** (not bold), about **4 % of frame height** (about 28 px at 720p), **2–7 characters each** (灵动岛, 防溅抗水, 4800万像素主摄).
- Placed in the negative space **above** the device, never on it, at y ≈ 37–49 %.
- **Hard cut on and hard cut off** with the shot or the beat. No animation.
- Hold times: 灵动岛 1.5 s, 防溅抗水 3.0 s, 2倍长焦 2.0 s, 4800万 2.0 s, 续航 1.0 s, "iPhone 15" 1.3 s. The logo replaces the name in place, with no transition.
- The real hero type is the **UI shot at macro**. Flighty green numerals sit at about 5 % of frame height and UI fills 40–60 % of frame width. **[verified 22.5 s: the Live Activity card fills about 70 % of frame width, fully legible, with a face soft behind it]**

**Camera.**
- A snorricam-style rig locked to the phone.
- The screen's top edge sits about **62–65 % down the frame**, tilted back **60–70°**, with the Dynamic Island centred. **[verified 7.8 s: top bezel at about 61 %, home-screen icons at headline size]**
- The macro glide opens the film: 14–18 mm-equivalent framing at near-zero height, with the bump rising over the horizon. **[verified 1.0 s]**
- One fly-by puts the device past the lens so the back fills 70 % of the frame (15–16.5).

**Light.**
- Large soft window light from the side. A thin rim of light on the aluminium edge. The screen is the brightest object in most frames.
- Background at about f/1.4: everything except the device is bokeh.
- Grade: warm amber mids, teal-green shadows, lifted blacks, filmic roll-off.
- The product colour (dusty pink) sits inside the grade and never fights it.

**Transitions.**
- Whip pans and whip rolls: **4–6 frames** of directional blur with 40–180° of roll. The swap happens at peak blur, and the incoming shot is already moving as it eases out over 8–10 frames.
- A hand, thumb or face passing the lens hides a world swap.
- A colour-light beat floods the frame red for 1.6 s with no change in composition (31.4–33.0).
- Push-through into the end world (34.5–37.3).

**Sound.**
- One upbeat track ("Wow"), −17.8 LUFS. Natural sound is minimal.
- Two deliberate breaths: 3.0 s (−46 dB) and 28–29 s (−31 to −33 dB).
- The music cuts out at 37.5 s (−34 → −71 dB) and **the logo sits in silence**.

### 02: Apple iPhone 15/16/17 spots (96.2 s, three films)

**Structure.** Each spot runs: a slow, mysterious reveal of material, then a beat-locked parade of product and UI, then a final hero hold. The spots are separated by black and silence.
- A, pink: 0–35.5.
- B, ultramarine: 37–63.
- C, lilac and lineup: 65.5–96.
- **There are no supers at all.**

**Edit rhythm.**
- 12 hard cuts, so the "ASL" is about 7.4 s. That number is misleading: most shot changes are continuous camera moves, object wipes and morphs.
- The opening macro section holds long: 0.7–8.4 has cuts at 2.73 / 4.7 / 8.37 that change only the angle, each about 2–4 s.
- Colour-swap wipes run at one colour per **0.6–0.8 s**, each on a beat.
- Island morphs take **about 0.2 s** each.
- Section C speeds up its cuts (82.2, 85, 89.8).

**Type.**
- None designed. All text is UI on a screen, in perspective, moving only because the device moves.
- UI text holds as long as its state does: incoming call about 1.2 s, flight Live Activity about 2.5 s, "9:41" in hand about 6 s.
- Scale comes from the camera, not the font: "9:41" fills about 70 % of frame height at 72.8–76 because the lens is close.

**Camera.**
- Always inside or against a material: grazing satin metal, skimming glass, riding a highlight. **[verified 8 s: pink metal at architectural scale, light seam on the horizon]**
- Very slow moves of about **2–4 % of frame per second** in the opening.
- Low grazing dollies along the edge, then a rise and orbit (17.2–25.5). **[verified 19 s: pink lens rings on seamless white, grazing angle]**
- Locked low angles for the object wipes. **[verified 26.5 s: a pink phone rises soft through the foreground over the yellow one]**
- A slow pull-back while the colour fan settles (89.7–96). **[verified 93 s]**

**Light.**
- Large soft sources. Speculars read as broad bands, never points.
- Light seams sit on horizons. Rim and edge highlights carry the drama.
- The light sweep has a white core, a peach halo and a violet/cyan fringe (45.2–47.5). **[verified 46.5 s: on ultramarine/near-black, which is a dark section]**
- Each section lives in one hue world (pink, ultramarine, lilac) on seamless white (#F7F7F8) or a single-colour field.

**Transitions.**
- Light-bloom match (15.0–17.5): exposure ramps up over 0.5 s (ease-in) and the frame holds near-white for **about 4 frames**. The next shot resolves through a focus pull from 30 px blur to 0, and both shots share a shape in the same screen position. **[verified 16.9 s: the corridor end blooms to white]**
- Foreground object wipes: 0.5–0.7 s with ease-out.
- Graphic match at 68.0: the lotus vanishing point lands between the two lenses.
- Fades to black separate the spots (35–37, 62.5–65.5). Those fades come from the compilation, not from the films.

**Sound.**
- −14.4 LUFS.
- A: an ambient −20 dB bed, a swell into the 17 s reveal, a plateau around −13 dB, a peak of −10 dB at 34 s.
- B: quieter (−25 to −30), with a **breath dip to −45 dB at 44.0** just before the 45.2 light-sweep hit.
- C: a dense, flat bed at −17 dB.

### 03: "Apple's best animation" (101.9 s, 30 fps)

**Structure.** Forty years of hardware told as **one unbroken morph chain**: one hard cut at 3.0 s, then 98.9 s with no cuts. Mac → click wheel → Touch ID → Face ID → … → Watch → XR colours → two closing lines → logo.
- Energy: a calm start, densest at 15–40 s (a morph about every 1.2 s), a breather at 44–50, dense again at 63–90, then a quiet type-and-logo ending (90–101.9 s, about 11 % of runtime).

**Edit rhythm (the morph is the unit).**
- Each transform takes **0.5–1.0 s**, then holds **0.8–1.5 s** on the finished object.
- The longest holds go to objects people know: iPod wheel about 1.4 s, XR back about 2.5 s.
- Inside a morph, each step is ease-in-out over **6–9 frames**. In the 22.0–22.6 example, four steps take about 0.8 s. **[verified 22.6 s: the ring has closed and the grey fill is arriving]**
- About 72 BPM, with an accent every 1.65 s. Big events land on strong accents: the bloom at 51.9, the heart at 73.5–74.4.

**Type.**
- Almost none. Object-native text ("hello.", MENU, "tv+") belongs to the objects.
- The closing two lines are a neutral sans, centred, black on grey, **one line at a time, held about 3 s each**. "Give people wonderful tools" then "And they'll do wonderful things", with **"wonderful" picked out in a darker weight** and the rest in mid-grey, then a crossfade.
- Words per card: 4–5.
- **[verified 93.5 s: the emphasis is exactly as described. Correction on size: the doc says about 1.6 % of frame height. On the frame the cap height is about 3 % and the full line height about 4 %. Still small.]**

**Camera.**
- Locked and centred almost throughout.
- The moves are motivated: a slow pull-out at the start, a zoom into the hole pattern and an expo-out back (22.8–24.5, about 1.2 s), a pull-out on the rosette, a pseudo-3D cover-flow dolly.

**Light.**
- Ground: light grey (about #DCDCDE) with a soft white radial vignette, like a lit studio sweep. **[verified]**
- Objects are white, mid-grey or black. **One accent family on screen at a time**, used only when the product owns it.
- Glow and bloom appear **only** at 13.4 (Face ID spin, gated by speed), 41 (Siri core), 51–52 (tv+ bloom) and the flashes at 23.0 and 59.5. **[verified 13.4 s: cyan glow wraps only the spinning ring]**
- **[correction 41.3 s: two screen-blended orbs (magenta and cyan) are visible mid-merge, not three]**

**Transitions.**
- No cuts. Everything is a carry-over morph: the survivors ease out into their new slots while the leavers ease in, accelerating out with blur and a slight scale-up.
- The white bloom grows over about 20 frames, peaks for about 3, and contracts over about 0.4 s to a dot that *is* the next element.

**Sound.**
- −14.6 LUFS. 0–3 s is silent.
- The bed holds a flat −14 to −20 dB the whole way and fades from 96 s.
- **Energy comes from how dense the morphs are, not from loudness.**

### 09: Apple-style single-element morph (14.03 s loop, 720², 30 fps)

**Structure.** One black UI object goes through 12 states with 0 cuts: button → loader → check → island → player → volume → toggle → segmented control → analytics card → ⌘K → toast → button. The loop is seamless, and a cursor causes every change.

**Edit rhythm.**
- A new state about every **1.1 s**. Each morph lasts about **0.2–0.3 s**.
- About 75 % of the time the viewer is reading; about 25 % they are watching a change.
- At 120 BPM, state changes land on the 8th-note grid (0.25 s): on-beat at 0.6, 1.1, 5.0, 7.1, 9.1, 12.6; off-beat at 1.3, 1.85, 3.8, 6.35, 9.8, 11.8.

**The morph recipe (numbers to copy).**
1. Anticipation: shrink about 5 % for 1 frame.
2. Stretch over 3–5 frames, with **accumulation motion blur**: 4–6 visible sub-copies, not a Gaussian. **[verified 1.33 s: horizontal ghosting at the pill's ends]**
3. Spring with damping ratio about 0.7–0.75, so it overshoots about 4–5 % and settles in about 200 ms. **[verified 1.97 s: the card at peak overshoot, title still blurred]**
4. Content blurs in from 8 px to 0 and fades from 0 to 1 over about 100 ms (3 frames), **staggered 1 frame (33 ms) per row**.
5. Total ≈ 0.23 s. Content is sharp by 0.3 s.
- Elastic indicator: the leading edge's spring is about 1.5× stiffer than the trailing edge's, so the pill stretches to about 1.6× its width mid-move.

**Type.**
- UI type only, Inter/SF-like. The semibold button label is about 3 % of the frame.
- Always inside the object; never floats on the ground.
- Labels hold 0.5–1.8 s. Labels change by blur-crossfade.

**Camera.** Locked and centred.

**Light.**
- Warm off-white ground (about #ECEBE6) with a soft radial vignette.
- A pure black object with one soft shadow: 20–30 px blur, about 15 % opacity, about 6 px down. The shadow scales with the morph.
- **The only colour is the album gradient.**

**Transitions.** None. Element correspondence does the work: one sub-shape survives every change (the progress bar becomes the volume track, the toggle knob becomes the segment indicator).

**Sound.** −14.1 LUFS, a transient every 1.0 s, a deliberately flat energy curve (it is a loop).

## 3. Principles that survive the Apple filter (ranked)

1. **The product is the protagonist; show its real UI at a scale you can read.** The UI shot at macro or at a grazing angle *is* the type (01 at 21–23 s, 02 at 72.8 s, 09). The camera supplies the scale, not the font size. Every feature gets one shot (01).
2. **Continuity: nothing appears from nowhere.** One sub-shape survives every change (09). Each object grows from a leftover of the last (03). Shots pass through each other in a bloom, a passing object or a whip, not a dissolve (01, 02, 03; also 10, 12).
3. **One idea per shot, then a hold.** Change for 0.2–1.0 s, then let the finished image rest 0.8–1.5 s (03). Viewers read about 75 % of the time and watch change about 25 % (09). One clear action per shot (06).
4. **Restraint in colour and effect.** One accent family at a time on a neutral ground (02, 03, 09). Glow appears only at speed or on a single event (03: 4 events in 99 s). The frame rests clean between moments (README rank 4).
5. **Light reveals the form.** Large soft sources, broad specular bands, a rim light on the edge, one moving sweep across a still object, a light seam on the horizon (01, 02). Contact shadows sell physical UI (10).
6. **Physical motion with one spring recipe everywhere.** Anticipation, a stretch with real sub-frame motion blur, about 5 % overshoot, a settle in about 200 ms (09). Blur only on fast moves; slow moves stay crisp (10). Real accumulation blur, not a filter (01, 02, 09, 12).
7. **Hold the camera on the device, move the world.** The device sits at about 63 % frame height, tilted 60–70°, while backgrounds swap (01). Grazing, slow dollies of 2–4 % of frame per second (02).
8. **Type is sparse, short, and cut on the beat.** Two to five words, one line at a time, in negative space, never as a label next to an object. Hard on/off or a blur-in, held 1–3 s (01, 03). Emphasis comes from weight inside one line (03's "wonderful"). For this film, keep the placement and holding logic but set it large enough to read on a phone.
9. **Silence is punctuation.** A breath of −45 dB before a hit (02 at 44.0; 01 at 3.0), and **the end mark held in silence** (01 at 37.5–39). Energy comes from density, not loudness (03). Mix at about −14 LUFS.
10. **Bookend.** Open and close on the same idea (01's surface glide and terrain end mark). Finish on calm and light: a white card, staggered lines, a 3.5 s hold over a decaying tail (11, 19.4–23.9).

## 4. What to drop

| Effect (refs) | Why it reads as "crazy" or as a template rather than Apple |
|---|---|
| RGB split, glitch, slice displacement (07; 08 "chroma") | Apple never breaks the image. A glitch says "effect", not "product" |
| Decode-scramble type (12) | A tech trailer cliché that turns words into noise. Apple type simply appears, or blurs in |
| Word-by-word "tinted to white" builds on every line (06) | Fine once, but on every headline it reads as a SaaS template |
| Giant figure-ground words at 35–65 % of frame height, outline/solid pairs (07) | It is poster-showreel energy. Apple's type is calm and small next to the product |
| HUD micro-labels, tickers, timecode, "×05 LAYERS" (07, 08 chrome) | Already rejected by the owner, and pure noise |
| Logo slam from the lens, mandalas, 300-tile fields, ring-click combination locks (05) | Envato template grammar; the dense fields are also trypophobic |
| Crossed light shafts, bloom on everything, heavy vignette, prop confetti of coins and gems (12) | Neon "hype" lighting. Apple uses soft, broad light and one event |
| Light-speed tunnels, laser and comet streaks, god-rays in space, jewel-setting carousels (11 middle) | K-pop teaser spectacle. The cuts every 0.3–0.6 s bury the meaning |
| Sunburst rays, halftone bursts, ASCII, mosaic, dither textures (06, 07, 08) | Pop-art and hacker looks; trypophobia risk |
| Spoiler-dust and particle bursts flying at camera; clouds of floating 3D icons (10) | The "AE template" signature. Apple shows one object, not dozens |
| Time-echo trails, mirrored ghosts, panel multiplication, synced grids (07) | Effect for its own sake; dilutes the subject |
| Felt or plush mascots, cartoon hand cursors (06) | Toy register. Apple shows a real hand or the UI reacting on its own |
| Constant bed at −9 dB or a −7.8 LUFS brick-wall master (06, 07) | No dynamics means no punctuation |
| Dark or black worlds as a setting (02 B section, 04, 07, 11, 12) | Known rejection. Darkness is allowed only as a sub-second dip |
| 01's flash frames, the under-car roll, the long red tunnel | Disorienting in a vertical frame; at most one colour-light beat |
| The glass-bubble "agent" that lenses and changes the UI (04, 10) | It is the old film's Bub idea in another form. Excluded unless the owner reopens it |
| Bub, his eyes, candy pastels, the old beat map and every "Steal for almost friends" list in 01–12 | **Excluded by brief** |

Keep with care: 08's light-theme silk field and about 3 % grain only as a near-still ground; 11's calm white end card; 10's slab UI and contact shadows; 06's zoom-through-the-button at most once.

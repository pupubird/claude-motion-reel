# almost friends — the energy curve and the WOW map

The owner: "I don't want linear all the way … design the curve well, every 1.5–2 s must have a WOW!"
Measured before this map: our preview had 4 big moments in 53 s (peak 2.9× the median); the special edition 9 (4.1×);
the v2 crazy cut 15 (6.3×). **Target: a WOW at least every 2 s (≥ 26), peak ≥ 6× median, one deliberate breath.**

One bar = 2.016 s (119 BPM). Every WOW lands on a downbeat or a beat. Each WOW is a different *kind* of move, so the
energy has texture, not just volume.

## The type system (replacing the baseline wipes)

- Every line is set as separate 3D letters (Chrome-kerned), animated per letter or per word.
- **Entrances:** fly out of depth (toward the camera → into place) with real motion blur, 3–4 frames between letters,
  expo-out with ~5% overshoot; or slam in on a downbeat with a camera kick.
- **Hierarchy:** step numerals huge (1 · 2 · 3 · 4); the key word of each line in the accent colour; ink by day.
- **Exits:** whip or fly out with motion blur, never a fade or a wipe-down.

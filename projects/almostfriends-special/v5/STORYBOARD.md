# almost friends — special edition

A 44 s vertical showreel cut of reel 06, made to show what the film's own material can do. The released film
(`projects/almostfriends`, tag `almostfriends-v1.0`) explains the app step by step; this cut is free to put craft
first: one continuous take, every transition a carry, a camera that is operated, real light and real motion blur,
and the interface itself made of liquid.

The owner's brief, verbatim: "go all out to rework on the special effects, scenes, transition, every single camera
movements, UI elements rework, this is about making an award winning showreel version that we can go flex to the
world! Do it for english version, refer to the link skill and the video references high quality" — then "if you
need to redesign a complete new version, can do a almostfriends-special/ with new music, new sound effects
everything", and "the reference video set the bar, the vibe and design you can keep the original version, just a
complete new that you get to decides everything, go as hard as you can, go as over as you can".

## The idea

**Bub is the interface.** Everything on screen is one material — soap film and Liquid Glass — so anything can
become anything the way liquid does: a droplet pulls out of Bub and flattens into the question, the question's
answers drip out as chips, a tapped chip rounds back into a drop and goes home, a ring Bub gathers becomes the
scan front, a message pulls out of the bottom of the frame as a drop, two bubbles touch and share a wall (the
brand's own mark), the wall bursts and they become one, every friend in the sky merges into the mark. And the
brand line is the shape of the film: it opens **inside your bubble** in the dark and the day pours in when it pops.

Two alternatives were weighed and dropped: a chain reaction of UI parts (every tap the collision that starts the
next — clever, but the material would have been generic UI) and a dive through scale (from a pixel of Bub's eye
out to the crowd — strong, but it would have spent the film on one move).

## The look

- **Two lights.** The brand's daylight (sky #DCEFFF → peach #FFE8DA) is the film's home; the night it opens in is
  the brand's ink taken toward black (#050B1E), and the wait goes to dusk. Night → day twice: the brand line,
  played as light.
- **Soap film** — real thin-film interference (Airy reflectance through the CIE 1931 observer, as a LUT), drained
  thin at the top, swirling; a bubble's film thins to gold and black before it bursts, as a real one does.
- **Liquid Glass** — the interface refracts what is behind it (more at the bevel, split by wavelength), frosted,
  with a specular lip, an inner edge light, a design shadow on the light ground.
- **Type in the scene** — the night's words live inside the bubble's film; the day's words sit behind the bubbles
  that cross them; the foam's lines sit on a glass card the foam refracts through; the name takes a passing light.

## The camera

Keyed per act and operated: a two-sine hand (zero in the holds), a shake on every landing, keys that lead what they
frame. The whip at 10.0 is an expo-out (all speed at once); the dive is an expo-in; the wait is one slow creep and
nothing else.

## The sound

- **Score:** ElevenLabs Music (music_v2_5), a 7-chunk composition plan on the film's grid (`tools/music.py`). Three
  takes; s3 chosen by measurement (`tools/score_check.py`): steady 120 BPM, strong hits at 2, 10, 16, 38, a
  breath at −36 dB from 22 to 28, the climax from 28. Its groove drops at 6.0, so the picture puts Bub's eyes there.
- **Effects:** 32 sounds made with ElevenLabs Sound Effects (`tools/sfx.py`), measured (`tools/sfx_meta.py`) and
  placed by `tools/mix.py` from the cue sheet (`src/cues.js`, built from the picture's timeline) at their transient,
  peak or end, panned to where they happen, in one synthetic room; the sub and the heartbeat are synthesised. No
  pitched UI sound: taps, pops, clicks, bloops, whooshes.
- **Master:** −14 LUFS integrated, −2 dBTP.

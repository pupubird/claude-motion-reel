# almost friends v2 — the crazy all-out version (treatment)

**The brief, in the owner's words, in order:** the client: "still too low quality, not 特效 enough, not cool enough"; the
owner: "要高级感，不一定要同一个design" · "现在的很低级" · "对标苹果" · on the Apple style frames (`out/lab_apple-sheet.jpg`):
"too simple, i want a crazy all out version try".

**What stays:** the released cut's clock, words and score (`src/score.js`, `../almostfriends/audio/mix.wav`): the owner
approved its tempo and message, and the score's hits are where the picture's events land.

**What changes:** everything you see. Premium execution (the Apple style frames' language: neutral grounds, colour only
in light, big tight Inter type, real glass and metal lit like a studio) pushed to spectacle: every beat gets a signature
set piece, and the whole film travels **from night into daylight** — a lonely dark at the question, light growing
through the search and the three days, full daylight from the reveal. *Make friends outside your bubble* = out into
the light.

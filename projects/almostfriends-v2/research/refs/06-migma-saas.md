## 2. Why it reads high-end

The reel commits to a **single, ownable colour world**: black at the top melting into burnt orange, with horizontal light streaks like a long-exposure sunset. Every shot sits on it, so 14 fast shots feel like one film. Every cut lands exactly on the beat grid (≈0.53 s). Inside each shot there is exactly **one clear action**: a word appears, a chip pops, a check passes, a button is pressed. Fast never turns into busy. The mascot is a tactile, **fuzzy-felt 3D material** (C4D velvet or fur shading), which gives the flat UI a physical, toy-like warmth. Its transitions are *semantic*: the cursor presses "Approve" and the camera dives into the button until its gradient becomes the next full frame.

## 4. Signature moves (特效)

### 4.1 Zoom-through-the-button transition (15.9–17.2)
- **Frame by frame:** the camera pushes from a full card (~60% of frame width) to the Approve button at ~3× in ~0.6 s with ease-in. A hand cursor arrives and the press happens on the beat: the button scales ~0.96 and its gradient lightens ~20% for ~6 frames, then rebounds. **Without stopping**, the camera keeps accelerating into the button until the pink→orange gradient covers the whole frame (17.1 still: the button's rounded edge is still visible at ~2× frame). It cuts on the beat to a full-frame gradient, which *is* the next scene's background.
- **Build:** one continuous scale/translate keyframe on the parent with an exponential ease-in (the scale curve accelerates, so it reads as "falling into" the button). The next scene starts with the same gradient and angle. In our pipeline it is a camera dolly in Three.js/CSS 3D with motion blur on the last 6 frames.
- **Why it's cool:** the UI action *causes* the transition. The viewer feels the click "open" the next scene.

### 4.2 Character swap inside a ring, then a ring-to-sunburst burst (1.6–3.77)
- **Frame by frame:** an orange glossy ring (thick bezel with a light-to-dark orange gradient, inner white-to-peach radial) scales up 0.3→1 with overshoot in ~10 frames. The character inside **spins on its Y axis**: at edge-on (2.0) it swaps to the next character, so each swap is a 180° turn over ~6 frames on the beat (2.1, 2.65). On 2.8–3.0 the ring gets a **zoom and radial blur** as if spinning up, then on the 3.1 hit it bursts into ~10 **soft orange sunburst wedges** (radial gradient from orange at the centre to transparent at the frame edge) rotating ~5°/s behind the final character (Felipe).
- **Build:** a 2D sprite or 3D character with `rotateY` and a texture swap at 90°. The ring is a CSS conic or radial gradient disc. The sunburst is a fragment shader: `w = step(0.5, fract(atan(uv.y,uv.x)/TAU*N + t*0.015))`, multiplied by `1 - smoothstep(0.2, 1.2, length(uv))`. Add a radial blur pass (sample along the vector to the centre) for the spin-up frames.
- **Why it's cool:** a candidate carousel compressed into 1.5 s, ending in a "winner" burst. That is literally our Bub-search-to-match beat.

### 4.3 Word-by-word "tinted to white" reveal (every headline)
- **Frame by frame:** each word enters with opacity 0→1, **blur ~6→0 px**, a ~10 px rise and **colour shifting from the background accent** (translucent burnt orange or dark rose) to pure white over ~6–8 frames. Words are staggered at **~0.23 s** (≈7 frames at 30 fps, one per 8th note). "Run your email marketing from Dots." = 6 words in ~1.4 s. The final word is often held tinted a beat longer ("Dots.", "friday.", "inbox.") before it whitens, so the payload word lands last.
- **Build:** per-word spans with `interpolate` on opacity, blur, translateY and colour, `start = lineStart + i * 7`. Trivial in our type system: per-word uniforms in the MSDF shader.
- **Why it's cool:** the words seem to condense *out of* the background colour rather than sitting on it. That avoids the "slapped-on type" look.

### 4.4 Streaked long-exposure background (whole film)
- Black or very dark brown at the top melts into burnt orange (#c4561a-ish) and then peach-cream at the bottom. **Horizontal smear bands** (like a motion-blurred photo of lights) drift slowly sideways (~20 px/s). On the white scenes the same streaks appear as soft orange bands on white.
- **Build:** a fragment shader. Vertical gradient LUT, plus `fbm(vec2(uv.x*0.4 + t*0.02, uv.y*28.0))` as an additive streak layer at 10–25% opacity, plus a slight film grain. It is cheap, and it unifies the cuts.
- **Why it's cool:** a living, textured backdrop stops flat UI from looking like a screenshot, and it is the brand's signature.

### 4.5 Unblur as "quality" reveal plus sequential checks (10.2–12.37)
- The email thumbnail starts as a ~20 px Gaussian blur, a colour blob only. As each checklist row flips from a grey spinner to a green "✓ Passed" pill (rows ~0.12 s apart, cascading top to bottom), the thumbnail sharpens step by step. When the last pill passes, it is fully crisp.
- **Build:** tie the blur radius to the count of passed rows. The pill is a scale 0.6→1 pop with a colour fill sweep.
- **Why it's cool:** an abstract claim ("enhance themselves") becomes a visible, physical change.

### 4.6 Chrome-swap continuity (12.37–15.07)
- One email holds its position and keeps scrolling while only the window chrome changes (title bar colour, client name, app icon). Each swap is a hard 1-frame change on a beat, so the effect is a "flip-book" of the same content. The dock icon of the current client lifts ~8 px and gets a coloured fill.
- **Why it's cool:** it proves "works everywhere" with zero explanation, and the rhythmic flip is satisfying.

### 4.7 Task chips bursting out of the phone (6.4–8.0)
- White rounded cards (soft shadow, icon tile, two-line text) emerge from *behind* the phone edge with strong horizontal motion blur (6.6 frames show streaked, blurred chips) and settle with a small overshoot. They float with a slight idle bob.
- **Build:** spring (stiffness ~180, damping ~14) on x from behind the phone mask, plus subframe blur.

### 4.8 Grid collage with Figma-like frame nodes (8.03–10.2)
- An infinite grid of white cells separated by 1 px black lines with ~10 px black dots at the intersections. Content cells are real designs (photos, product layouts) panning inside their masks. The headline fragments type into empty cells in a heavy condensed sans at ~15% of frame height. Rows slide past each other on beats.
- **Build:** a CSS grid or canvas with per-row translateX on beats and per-cell `object-position` pans.

### 4.9 Felt / plush mascot material (throughout)
- The characters are soft, fuzzy, subsurface-y blobs (velvet sheen on the rim, matte core, slightly noisy silhouette), with simple black bead eyes and props (beret, glasses, bow tie). They are rendered in C4D with a fur or velvet shader.
- It is not ours to copy (Bub is a soap bubble), but the lesson is that **a tactile, non-flat material on the mascot** carries the whole warmth of the piece.

## 5. Type

- **Family:** a **heavy condensed grotesk** (Druk Text / Neue Haas Display Condensed class), bold, tight tracking (≈ −2%), sentence case with a full stop at the end of every line ("Dot reports back.", "Scheduled for friday."). UI text is a neutral sans (SF Pro / Inter).
- **Placement:** headline centred near the top third (y ≈ 15–20%) with the subject (UI, card, phone) below it. In the collage, words live **inside grid cells** as part of the layout. In the outro, words are centred and stacked.
- **Scale:** headlines are ~7–9% of frame height. The collage words are ~15%. Outro words are ~10%.
- **Entry:** word by word with blur and the tinted-to-white shift (4.3). Logo text types character by character.
- **Exit:** mostly by hard cut on the beat. In the outro stack, the previous word dims to ~50% and shifts up a line.
- **Hold:** after the last word lands, lines hold **0.6–1.2 s** before the cut ("Run your email marketing from Dots." fully readable ~0.8 s; "Scheduled for friday." ~0.7 s; "Works in every inbox." ~2.2 s while the chrome swaps beneath it).

## 6. Colour, light, material

- **Palette:** black (#050302), burnt orange (#c9581d), peach (#f6c7a4), cream-white. The accent gradient is hot pink (#e5306d) to orange (#f29a3b) on buttons and the "Scheduled" field. The red-magenta logo has a glow. Felipe's blue (#2a8cf0) is the only cool colour, and it pops hard against the orange.
- **Light:** soft and even on the UI (no harsh speculars). The background supplies the glow (a bottom-up warm "horizon" light). The logo gets a bloom flare on reveal.
- **Materials:** glossy pill buttons with a subtle gradient. Felt and velvet characters. Phones are a warm copper/orange titanium frame catching the background colour. Shadows are soft and large-radius.

## 7. Tempo & sound

- Driving track, ~113 BPM. **Cuts sit on a ≈0.53 s beat grid** to within a frame or two (1.6 + n×0.533 predicts 3.73, 4.80, 8.00, 10.1, 25.07). Micro-actions (word reveals, chip pops, check pills, chrome swaps) fall on 8th notes (~0.25 s).
- **Energy curve:** high and constant from 0.5 s to 20.8 s, a **breakdown at 20.8–22.9** (the "Made to convert" insight plays quieter and slower, with a long push), then **re-entry on the 23.0 hit** for "Design. Send. Track." and the logo. That single dip-and-return gives the 27 s reel a shape.
- The SFX feel is mostly the music, with clicks implied by the hand-cursor presses landing on snare hits.

## 8. Steal for almost friends

1. **Bub search: candidate swap inside a lens ring, then a sunburst on the match.** The current search (15–21.5 s) is a dense pink and green bubble field where Bub drifts and a gold ring appears late. Instead, put Bub's search "lens" (a glossy ring, our blue→coral gradient bezel) centre-frame and **swap candidate bubble-faces inside it on each 8th note** with a Y-spin (swap at edge-on). Three or four swaps run 0.25 s apart, accelerating, then the ring gets a radial spin blur and **bursts into soft coral sunburst wedges** behind the matched bubble on the hit. This replaces the cluttered field with one readable, rhythmic action.
2. **Unlock: zoom through the yellow Unlock button.** The current unlock (41–50 s) shows a small padlock inside the phone UI and then the faces appear. Instead, after the tap on "Unlock" (cursor squash on the beat), the camera **keeps accelerating into the yellow button until its gradient fills the frame**. Cut on the beat to a full-frame warm yellow→coral field where a big white padlock **draws on and clicks open** (stroke draw ~8 frames, shackle springs up with overshoot) and "You're both in." builds word by word.
3. **Reveal: unblur as the payoff.** The current 50.5–51 s goes from bubbles straight to crisp face circles with confetti. Instead, the two faces start **behind frosted bubble glass at ~24 px blur** (we already raymarch the bubbles, so blur the refracted sample) and **sharpen step by step on 3 beats** as "Day 1 / Day 2 / Day 3" ticks pass, ending crisp on the unlock hit. Like the "enhance themselves" checklist, this makes the reveal a visible physical change.
4. **All headlines: tinted-to-white word builds.** The current "New friends who share your values." (3.5–6.5 s) and "Pick what matters to you" lines pop in as solid ink and blue. Instead, each word condenses out of the background (starting as translucent sky-blue or peach at ~6 px blur, rising ~12 px, turning to full ink or gradient over 8 frames at 60 fps ×2 = 14–16 frames). Stagger one word per 8th note, and hold the payload word ("values.", "friends.") tinted a beat longer.
5. **Brand field: our own streaked long-exposure background.** The current pale sky→peach gradient is static and flat. Add a shader layer of **horizontal light streaks drifting sideways** (fbm stretched 1:30, 10–20% opacity, blue streaks in the upper sky and coral in the lower peach) plus fine grain. Keep it pale and airy (no black). It unifies the cuts and stops the background from reading as a default gradient.
6. **Values pick: chips burst out of the phone.** The current values chips (9.5–11 s) sit inside the phone screen at small size. Have the three chosen value chips **fly out from behind the phone's edges** with horizontal motion blur and spring settle (stiffness ~180, damping ~14), floating outside the device at 2–3× legible size, one per beat.
7. **Chat days: chrome-swap continuity.** The current Day 1 / Day 2 / Day 3 (34.5–39.5 s) is three similar holds with a moving sun glow. Keep the chat bubble column fixed and **swap only the "Day N" header and the time-of-day tint** of the background on hard beat cuts (morning blue → golden → dusk peach) while new bubbles slide in continuously. That makes it a flip-book rhythm instead of three holds.
8. **Outro stack: "Same values. / New friends. / Almost friends."** The current 57–60 s cross-fades "Same values. New friends." and then "Friendship is the whole point." Instead, use the stacked type-on (previous line dims to ~50% and shifts up a line on each hit) **over a 2 s music breakdown**, then re-enter on the hit with the end mark. That copies Migma's dip-and-return energy shape.

## 9. Don't copy

- **The black-to-burnt-orange world.** It is Migma's brand, and dark backgrounds are a known rejection for us. Rebuild the *streak texture* in our pale sky/peach palette.
- **The heavy condensed grotesk.** Our brand type is rounded and friendly. Steal the build behaviour, not the face.
- **The cartoon white hand cursor.** It reads as a clip-art meme. Use a ring or tap ripple in our colours instead.
- **The dense email-design grid collage.** In 9:16 with friend photos it would become a tiled face-grid, edging toward the trypophobic dense fields we've been told to avoid.
- **Dashboard stat panels and "Checking / Passed" lists as literal UI.** These lean toward HUD and micro-label territory. Take only the cascade timing and the unblur idea.
- **Plush or felt mascots.** Bub is a soap bubble. His material story is refraction, iridescence and wobble, not fur.

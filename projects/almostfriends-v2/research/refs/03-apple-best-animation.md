**What it is:** a 40-year history of Apple hardware and UI told as one unbroken morph chain. Each product turns into the next through a shared shape: Mac → click wheel → home button → Touch ID → Face ID → check → mice → iMac G3 → iMac G4 → Mac Pro → … → Watch → iPhone XR colours → "Give people wonderful tools / And they'll do wonderful things" → logo.

## Why it reads high-end

The shot never cuts. Every new object grows out of a leftover piece of the old one: a ridge, a dot, a ring or a line. Your eye never has to look for the subject again, so 99 s feels like one thought. The motion is physical. Things spin fast with real motion blur, settle on an ease-out, then hold still for about 1 s, so every morph reads as a finished, satisfying click. The palette is almost silent (light grey, white, one accent colour per object), so each colour that arrives is an event. There is no type until the last 10 s, so the objects carry the story by themselves. What makes it look expensive is **continuity plus restraint**, not added effects.

## Signature moves (特效)

### 1. The carry-over morph (every transition)
- **When:** all of it. The clearest examples are 4.3 (Mac → disc), 12.2 (fingerprint → Face ID), 22.0 (sticks → ring → disc) and 35.5 (line → cable).
- **Frame by frame (22.0–22.6, 10 fps strip):** five black dashes rotate as a pinwheel → they slide tangentially and line up on a circle (22.3) → the gaps close (22.5) → the ring fills grey (22.6–22.8) → the stroke fades. Four steps take about 0.8 s, and each step is an ease-in-out lasting about 6–9 frames at 30 fps.
- **Construction:** every object is a set of parametric strokes and fills (SVG paths / SDF primitives). The morph interpolates the parameters (position, rotation, arc length, corner radius, fill alpha) of the *same* primitives. It does not crossfade two drawings. In our WebGL world: one raymarched SDF scene where `smin(k)` blends, radii and centres are animated between keyframed "products". Bub, the value orbs, the crowd, the lock and the end mark should all be **the same SDF bodies re-parameterised**.
- **Why it's cool:** the viewer's eye never loses the subject, and every arrival feels earned because it was built from what was already there.

### 2. Unwind-and-reassemble (fingerprint → Face ID)
- **When:** 12.2–12.9 s.
- **Frame by frame:** 12.2: the outer ridges detach as separate arcs. 12.3: they fly outward and rotate. The largest arc leaves the frame top-left within about 3 frames, thickening as it goes as if it were coming toward the camera. 12.4–12.5: inner ridges break into short dashes and dots. 12.6: about 6 strokes remain and slide into eye / nose / mouth / corner positions. 12.8: the Face ID glyph is complete, still pink. 12.9: colour shifts to blue over about 4 frames.
- **Construction:** stroke `dashoffset` and segment splitting, with outward radial velocity and an ease-in (accelerating) for the leavers. Survivors use an ease-out into their slots. Leavers get brief directional blur and a slight scale-up (fake z).
- **Why it's cool:** the explosion and the rebuild happen in one gesture, so the transformation reads as *intelligence*, not editing.

### 3. Spin-to-confirm with glow (Face ID → check)
- **When:** 13.3–13.9 s.
- **Frame by frame:** 13.3: the cyan rounded square breaks into a dashed ring. 13.4: the ring spins on its Y axis at full speed. A **cyan outer glow plus motion-blur smear** wraps the blue stroke ([frame](frames/03-apple-best-animation-13.4s.jpg)). 13.5: it decelerates and the blur drops. 13.6–13.7: two crossed ellipses (a gyroscope), the last wobble. 13.8: a clean circle. 13.9: the check draws (about 4 frames). Then a 0.6 s hold.
- **Construction:** a 3D ring (torus SDF or line in perspective) with rotation that is fast in the middle and has a long ease-out tail (expo-out). The glow is a bloom pass gated by angular velocity: glow and blur exist *only* while it is fast.
- **Why it's cool:** velocity-gated glow says "processing… done". It is the only glow in the sequence, so it lands.

### 4. Orbit with hairline trace (mice → iMac G3 pentagon)
- **When:** 15.5–18.0 s.
- **Frame by frame:** the white and black mice orbit the centre on opposite sides. A 1 px grey arc trails behind each one for about 90° and fades ([frame](frames/03-apple-best-animation-16.8s.jpg)). The mice show slight directional blur. At 16.4 five tiny coloured triangles appear at the centre and scale up over about 0.6 s into the iMac G3 shells, which rotate slowly as a pentagon. The mice exit along their orbits.
- **Construction:** polar motion with a constant angular velocity, plus a trail made from the last N positions as a fading polyline. Children spawn at scale 0.05 with a staggered ease-out-back (slight overshoot, about 5%).
- **Why it's cool:** the hairline turns the motion into *drawing*. It shows intent and path, which is exactly what "Bub searches" needs.

### 5. Three-orb core + spirograph rosette (Siri → HomePod)
- **When:** 40.0–43.4 s.
- **Frame by frame:** 40.0–40.9: the horizontal Siri waveform, with cyan / pink / green lobes swelling and shrinking along a hairline. 41.0: the lobes pinch into **three translucent discs** (blue, red, green) overlapping with a screen-type blend. 41.2–41.5: they spin round a common centre with heavy motion blur and merge into one soft magenta-cyan core ([frame](frames/03-apple-best-animation-41.3s.jpg)). At the same time a thin white circle expands past the frame and spawns **radial spokes, then a rosette of hundreds of overlapping hairline circles** (a spirograph). 42.0–43.4: the camera pulls out and the rosette tightens into the HomePod mesh around the small glowing core.
- **Construction:** three gaussian-edged discs with additive/screen blending, rotated with blur (temporal supersampling or radial blur), then collapsed by radius → 0. The rosette is N≈60–120 circles of radius R whose centres sit on a circle of radius r, rotated by i·(2π/N) and drawn with 1 px alpha 0.3 strokes. Animating r from 0 to R gives the "bloom".
- **Why it's cool:** it is the AI-assistant visual idiom, and it shows computation as beauty: a living core plus geometry that grows from it.

### 6. White bloom as the transition (tv+ → Find My)
- **When:** 51.0–52.6 s; same idea at 23.0 and 59.5.
- **Frame by frame:** 51.0–51.9: the "tv+" letters go white-hot with a soft halo. 52.0: a radial white bloom grows from the centre to about 70% of the frame ([frame](frames/03-apple-best-animation-52.2s.jpg)). It peaks for about 3 frames, then contracts over about 0.4 s to a tiny blue dot (52.5), which grows into the next icon (52.7–52.9, ease-out-back).
- **Construction:** additive radial gradient with an animated radius and exposure. Everything is luma-based, with no colour.
- **Why it's cool:** it hides a topology change (text → icon) inside light, and it lands on a music accent (51.9).

### 7. Zoom-through-texture reveal (Mac Pro)
- **When:** 22.8–24.5 s.
- **Frame by frame:** the grey disc sits in a white field → the frame floods white → we are *inside* a hole pattern (big grey dots) → the zoom eases out (expo-out over about 1.2 s) while more dots enter → the dots become the Mac Pro grille → handles and feet appear → it turns 90°.
- **Construction:** a single scale on a tiled pattern, with the outline revealed at the end.
- **Note:** a dense hole field is a **known rejection** for us (trypophobia). We keep the *grammar* (one element → is a cell of a bigger thing → pull back), not the dot field.

### 8. Colour fan → radial spokes (iPhone XR lineup)
- **When:** 87.0–90.4 s.
- **Frame by frame:** a single coral phone back. 87.5: copies peel from behind it one per frame, each rotated about 8° further, forming a fan of 8 colours ([frame](frames/03-apple-best-animation-88.4s.jpg)). 88.0–88.4: the fan keeps rotating into a full circle. 88.5: each phone turns edge-on (scaleX → 0.1), leaving coloured **spokes** in a radial ring. 89–90: the spokes rotate slowly as a loading spinner, then fade.
- **Construction:** instanced quads with index-staggered rotation and a delay (about 1 frame per index), then a per-instance Y rotation to edge-on.
- **Why it's cool:** "one becomes many" in about 1.5 s, and the end state is a graphic emblem rather than a pile of copies.

### 9. 3D cover-flow out of a grid (app icons)
- **When:** 60.0–63.4 s ([frame](frames/03-apple-best-animation-62.6s.jpg)).
- The icons jiggle (±2° wobble with × badges). The red outline card comes forward while the others fold into perspective on either side as cover-flow, then the stack slides. Outline-only cards with pastel strokes make the 3D read in a very light way.

## Type

- **Almost none.** "hello." (script, on the Mac screen at 3.0 s, about 18% of frame width) is the object's own content. "MENU", ⏮⏭, "tv / tv+" belong to the objects.
- The closing lines (90.5–96 s) are tiny, about 1.6% of frame height in a neutral sans, centred, black on grey. Each holds about 3 s. "Give people wonderful tools" builds on, with "wonderful" picked out in a darker weight at 93.0, then crossfades to "And they'll do wonderful things".
- Takeaway for us: the *picture* carries the message; type arrives only when the picture can't say it. Our client wants big, legible type, so we borrow the *restraint of timing* (one line at a time, a long hold) but not the tiny size.

## Colour, light, material

- **Ground:** light grey (about #DCDCDE) with a soft white radial vignette that is lighter at the centre. It reads like a lit studio sweep, not a flat fill.
- **Objects:** white with a faint shadow, mid grey, black. Accent colour is used only when the product owns it (Touch ID pink, Face ID blue, iMac G3 flavours, Siri cyan/magenta/green, Find My green, Activity red/green/cyan, XR colours). One accent family is on screen at a time.
- **Light:** almost flat vector shading, with gradient sweeps used *as motion* (click-wheel build, 5.0–6.0). Glow/bloom appears only at 13.4 (Face ID spin), 41 (Siri core), 51–52 (tv+ bloom) and 23.0 / 59.5 (flashes).
- **Material:** none in a 3D sense; it is 2D vector with soft-shadow hints. This matters: **the "特效" here is choreography, not rendering.** Our raymarched glass bubbles are already richer in material. What we lack is this choreography.

## Tempo & sound

- Steady medium tempo (about 72 BPM, accent every 1.65 s). The music level is flat across the piece; energy comes from **morph density**, not loudness.
- Typical rhythm: a 0.5–1.0 s transform, then a 0.8–1.5 s hold on the finished object. The longest holds are on icons people know (iPod wheel about 1.4 s, XR back about 2.5 s).
- Strong accents get the "big" events: the white bloom (51.9), Find My at full size (53.5), heart drawing (73.5–74.4), watch grid (80.8).
- Energy curve: a calm start → densest at 15–40 s (a morph every ~1.2 s) → a slight breather at 44–50 → dense again at 63–90 → a long quiet type-and-logo ending (90–101 s, about 11% of runtime).

## Steal for almost friends

Our current release (sheets in `research/current-special/`) uses separate set-pieces joined by dissolves, whips and phone pops: Bub hook → logo burst (2.0) → type card (3.5) → white void → phone (7.0) → bubble world (15) → pink whip-blur (22.5) → phone (23) → … → dissolve to faces (50.5). The client's "not 特效 enough" is largely about these **joins**.

1. **One continuous morph chain, no dissolves** (all beats). Rebuild the film as one SDF world where each beat's hero is re-parameterised from the last. *Change vs current:* the 0:22.0–22.5 pink blur-whip into the phone, the 0:06.5 fade into an empty white void with a tiny phone, and the 0:50 dissolve all become shape-carried morphs. For example, the matched pair of bubbles *becomes* the two avatar orbs on the phone screen, with the phone assembling *around* them, instead of cutting to a phone that already contains them.
2. **Values pick → three-orb core (Siri move #5)** (values pick → Bub search, 0:12–0:15). The three chosen value dots (coral / blue / yellow, already on screen at 0:12) leave the UI, orbit Bub, merge into Bub's core as a spinning screen-blended triad with motion blur, and Bub's iridescence picks up the three colours. Bub then emits a **spirograph rosette of hairline rings** as its search pulse. *Change vs current:* this replaces 0:14.0–14.5, where flat concentric rings sit around a static orb inside the phone.
3. **Hairline orbit traces for the search** (Bub search, 0:15–0:21). As Bub darts through the crowd it leaves a 1–2 px white path that fades after about 0.5 s, and candidate bubbles it rejects get a short orbit-arc trace. *Change vs current:* Bub currently drifts through a busy pink field and its path is unreadable. The trace makes "searching" visible and gives the frame a designed line layer on top of the liquid.
4. **Spin-to-confirm with velocity-gated glow (move #3)** (match lock-in, about 0:20–0:21). When Bub finds the match, a ring spins up around the match bubble on its Y axis with a blue→coral glow that exists only while it is fast (about 18 frames at 60 fps at peak), then decelerates on expo-out into a clean ring and a checkmark/heart stroke draw (about 8 frames). *Change vs current:* this replaces the static yellow outline ring at 0:20–0:21.5.
5. **White bloom as the cut-hider (move #6)** (match → SAME!! phone, about 0:22; and unlock → reveal, about 0:50). Use a radial white bloom (grows over about 20 frames, peaks for 6, contracts over about 24 frames to a dot) timed to a music accent. The contracting dot *is* the next element (the phone's avatar dot / the first face). *Change vs current:* this replaces the pink smear frame at 0:22.5 and the faded phone at 0:50.0.
6. **Unwind-and-reassemble for the unlock (move #2)** (unlock → reveal, 0:49–0:51). The padlock's shackle and body strokes unwind into arcs that fly outward with blur. The surviving strokes slide into two circular face frames, and the photos iris in inside them. *Change vs current:* this replaces the UI padlock flipping open inside a card (0:49.5), followed by a dissolve.
7. **Fan → ring → emblem for "friends gather" (move #8)** (friends, 0:56–1:02). From the matched pair, face discs peel off one per 2 frames into a fan, the fan swings into a full ring around the pair, and the ring tightens into the end mark's two-bubble lockup. *Change vs current:* this replaces 0:57–1:01, where faces drift in randomly and the frame looks like a scattered collage.
8. **Hold discipline** (every beat). After each morph lands, hold for 0.8–1.2 s with only micro-motion. *Change vs current:* several beats move constantly with no resting pose (bubble world at 0:15–0:21; the phone scale-pops at 0:27.5 and 0:39.5), so nothing reads as a finished image.

## Don't copy

- **The flat 2D vector render.** The reference gets away with it because it's Apple nostalgia. For us, flat equals "low quality". Keep our raymarched glass/liquid and add this choreography on top.
- **The grey studio ground** (about #DCDCDE) for long stretches. Ours should stay our pastel blue→peach sky.
- **The Mac Pro perforation field** (23.0–24.5). It is a dense hole pattern, our known trypophobic rejection. Use the zoom-through grammar only with a few large spheres.
- **Tiny centred closing type** (about 1.6% height). Our brief demands big, legible type.
- **The 3 s silent black quote card** and the letterbox bars. A dead opening breaks our "frame 0 moving" rule.
- **The single-object pacing over 99 s.** We have 66 s and a product story, so compress transforms to 0.4–0.7 s.

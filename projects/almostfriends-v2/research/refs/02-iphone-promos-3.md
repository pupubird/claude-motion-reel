## Why it reads high-end

The camera is always inside or right against a material: grazing a satin metal surface, skimming a glass ring, riding the light sweeping along a phone edge. Shallow depth of field and huge scale make a phone feel like architecture. Shots almost never cut. They pass through each other: a corridor becomes a phone edge, a pink phone wipes past to reveal a yellow one, the vanishing point of a lotus wallpaper lands exactly in the gap between two lenses. Each section lives in one hue world (pink, ultramarine, lilac) on either a seamless white or a single-colour light field, and light is the main event: blooms, light seams and sweeping highlights with warm cores and chromatic fringes. UI moments such as the Dynamic Island and live activities use the same blur-in-transit spring morphs as reference 09, so software and hardware feel like one material.

## Signature moves (特效)

### 1. Macro "material architecture" opening
- **Timestamps:** 0.7–17.0
- **What happens:** the phone's own geometry (camera plateau, lens rings, frame curve) is shot so close and so wide that it reads as a pink marble or satin building. Surfaces are satin anodised metal with a broad soft specular. A thin hot light seam sits on the horizon line where the floor meets the far wall, and prismatic orange/violet fringes appear at the brightest edges (frame 8 s). The foreground is out of focus, so DoF suggests miniature scale inverted. The camera moves at a very slow ~2–4 % of frame per second.
- **Build:** raymarched SDF scene (rounded boxes and cylinders, smooth-unioned) with a GGX satin BRDF, a large emissive slab behind the horizon for the seam, depth-based bokeh blur (gather DoF), and a slight chromatic split (R/B offset proportional to luminance at the highlights). In our film: fly through **the inside of Bub's soap film**, iridescent thin-film interference instead of pink satin. Alternatively, a Higgsfield/Seedance shot "macro camera gliding over the surface of a giant iridescent soap bubble, light seam on horizon" admitted as media.
- **Why it reads cool:** it withholds the object. You are inside a beautiful space before you know what it is, and the reveal at 17 s pays it off.

### 2. Light-bloom match transition
- **Timestamps:** 15.0–17.5 (corridor → white haze → phone edge); 46.5–47.5 (edge highlight → full flare)
- **Frame by frame (15.5–17.5 at 10 fps):** the camera pulls down a symmetric corridor. The glass object at the end brightens to clipping (16.3), the bloom radius grows until it fills ~60 % of the frame with prismatic edges (16.8), and the whole frame becomes a pink-white haze for ~4 frames (16.9–17.1). The haze resolves into a blurred vertical pillar (the phone's lens column, 17.1–17.2), and the focus pulls sharp onto the real phone edge over seamless white by 17.5.
- **Build:** drive the exposure and bloom threshold of the first scene up and over 0.5 s (ease-in), hold ~4 frames at near-white, then crossfade under the white into the next scene while its exposure comes down and the DoF pulls from 30 px blur to 0. The match works because both scenes share **pink metal + vertical cylinder** at the same screen position.
- **Why:** the cut is hidden inside light, so it reads as one continuous camera move between two scales.

### 3. Foreground object wipes (colour swaps)
- **Timestamps:** 25.5–28.5 (pink → yellow → green → blue → black), 51.5–55.5 (lens close-ups), 68.0 (lilac back over lotus), 89.7 (black phone over the poster)
- **Frame by frame (25.5–28.5):** the camera is locked, low behind the camera bump. The next-colour phone rises from below frame, nearer the lens and therefore larger and soft, and slides up until it fully occludes the previous one. Each wipe takes ~0.5–0.7 s with an ease-out and slight DoF on the foreground body.
- **Build:** plain z-ordered 3D objects moving through the camera. In code: instance the next object closer to the camera on an easeOutCubic path.
- **Why:** you get the energy of a cut with the continuity of a camera move, and the colour change itself becomes the beat.

### 4. Dynamic Island / live-activity morphs
- **Timestamps:** 29.5–34.0, 80–81.5, 82.2–85
- **What happens:** the same spring morph as reference 09. The black capsule stretches into the call card over ~5 frames, with content blurred during transit (31.5 and 31.7 are blurred), a slight overshoot, then contacts and buttons fade up. Collapse is the reverse, with content blurring out first (33.3–33.5).
- **Build:** see 09 move 1 (SDF rect spring plus accumulation blur plus blur-fade content).

### 5. Light sweep along the edge
- **Timestamps:** 45.2–47.5 (frame 46.5)
- **Frame by frame:** the camera grazes along the ultramarine aluminium edge. A highlight band enters from the top left (45.8) and travels along the screen bezel. The band has a white core, a warm orange-peach halo and a violet/cyan outer fringe. The camera tilts over the edge into the light, so the flare covers ~40 % of the frame (46.5–47.5).
- **Build:** a moving area light, or an analytic anisotropic highlight on a cylinder SDF. In post: threshold → anamorphic streak plus 3-scale bloom, colour-graded so the core is white, the mid tones go orange and the far halo goes violet.
- **Why:** the light reveals form, and it feels expensive because it moves on its own across a still object.

### 6. Light bowl to wallpaper
- **Timestamps:** 37–42 (frame 39)
- **What happens:** from black, a blue glow rises into a luminous elliptical basin: a soft-edged ellipse with a bright cyan floor and darker blue walls. It deepens into nested bowls that turn out to be the iOS 18 wallpaper on the phone screen.
- **Build:** a 2D SDF ellipse field with smooth gradients and an inner-glow falloff `exp(-d*k)`, animated k, plus a fine noise grain. It is cheap in a fragment shader.

### 7. Action Button icon carousel
- **Timestamps:** 76.5–79
- **What happens:** glyphs slide horizontally through the physical button, and the one inside the button scales up ~1.6× and turns saturated violet. The ones outside are smaller and flatter.
- **Build:** a horizontal list with scale and opacity as a function of distance from the slot centre (fisheye), eased steps on the beat.

### 8. Colour fan finale
- **Timestamps:** 89.7–96 (frame 93)
- **What happens:** the hero phone lies diagonally in front. Four colour variants rotate out behind it around a common pivot, ~8° apart, and the camera pulls back as the fan settles with an ease-out.
- **Build:** N instances with rotation = i·θ(t), θ easing 0 → 8° over ~1.5 s, with a soft contact shadow.

## Type

- **There is no headline type at all.** All text is UI: lock-screen clock "9:41", caller name, flight times, the "RADIO SOUND CLUB" poster. It lives on a screen, is in perspective with the device, and moves only because the device moves.
- UI text holds as long as the UI state does: the incoming call holds ~1.2 s, the flight live activity ~2.5 s, "9:41" in hand ~6 s.
- Scale comes from the camera: the "9:41" numerals fill ~70 % of the frame height at 72.8–76 because the camera is close, not because the type is huge.

## Colour, light, material

- **A:** monochrome pink world (dusty rose #C48A9A shadows → blush #F2D7DE highlights), white light seams, satin anodised metal, frosted glass. Then seamless white (#F7F7F8) for the product shots, with each phone colour as the only colour.
- **B:** ultramarine/indigo (#1B1F6B → #4B5BE0) with a cyan-white light core and warm orange-peach flares. Darker, but always with a luminous centre.
- **C:** lilac/violet lotus (#6B5BD6 → #D9CDEE) on white, then a neutral white lineup.
- **Key light:** large soft sources. The specular reads as broad bands, not points. The rim and edge highlights carry the drama. Reflections on the floors are glossy but blurred.

## Tempo & sound

- **A** builds: an ambient −20 dB bed for 0–8 s, a swell into the 17 s reveal, then a beat-driven plateau around −13 dB with colour swaps on beats and a peak at 34 s (−10) right before the fade.
- **B** is quieter (−25 to −30) and darker, with a **breath dip to −45 dB at 44.0** right before the 45.2 light-sweep cut. The silence makes the hit land.
- **C** jumps to a dense, compressed bed at a flat −17 dB from 71 s. The cuts get faster (82.2, 85, 89.8), and object motion such as the icon steps, stack and fan carries the beat.
- Shared pattern: slow, mysterious material reveal → beat-locked product/UI montage → final hero hold. Each spot is separated by black and silence.

## Steal for almost friends

1. **Hook: open inside the material.** The current 0:00–0:01.5 is flat text "How to make more friends" over a cartoon-eyed bubble on a pale field. Instead, open on a macro glide across the iridescent soap-film surface of Bub at architectural scale: thin-film rainbow bands, a white light seam on the horizon, shallow DoF. Then pull out (light-bloom match, move 2) to reveal it is Bub's face, with the hook words in the film's reflection. Frame 0 is already moving.
2. **Use light-bloom matches instead of dissolves.** Replace the current pink whiteout at 0:22.5 (match → phone) and the blur smears at 0:06.5 and 0:43.5 with a deliberate bloom match. Exposure ramps for 0.5 s, the frame holds near-white for 4 frames, and the next shot resolves through a focus pull with **a shared shape at the same screen position**: Bub's round body → the round avatar in the match card; the two merged bubbles → the "You / Curious Otter" pair.
3. **Bub search: foreground object wipes.** The 0:15–0:22 crowd currently drifts in place. Instead, a locked low camera, with large out-of-focus bubbles rising through the foreground on easeOut paths and each one wiping to a new candidate behind (move 3). Use 4 wipes on 4 beats, then the match is revealed on the 5th.
4. **SAME!!: grow it out of the Dynamic Island.** The current 0:32 "SAME!!" is a sticker pasted over the phone. Instead, the island stretches open (move 4) into a full-width coral/blue capsule reading "SAME!!", with an overshoot and accumulation motion blur, then collapses back.
5. **Unlock: light sweep.** Currently (0:49–0:50) a yellow ring pulses around a small padlock. Instead, on the unlock hit a light band sweeps along the iPhone frame edge and over the padlock shackle (move 5): white core, peach halo, violet fringe, anamorphic streak. Precede it with a 0.4 s breath dip in the music, as at 44.0 here.
6. **Values pick: hue world per section.** The release uses the same pale blue-peach wash everywhere. Give each act one hue world built from the brand pair: blue-led for the values pick, coral-led for the match/chat, and blending blue+coral for the unlock/reveal, each with a luminous centre (move 6 light bowl), on a light field, not navy.
7. **Friends: colour fan.** The 0:57–1:01 avatar field is a dense scatter of small faces. Instead, use 5–6 friend cards or bubbles that fan out around a pivot behind the matched pair (move 8), ~8° apart, easing over 1.5 s while the camera pulls back. It is fewer, bigger faces and reads premium.
8. **Keep the camera moving.** Every current phone shot (0:08–0:14, 0:28–0:44) is a near-static, front-on phone. Use the grazing low angles and slow lateral dollies from 17–25 s: the phone edge in the foreground, the screen at 20–35° perspective, and DoF on the near frame.

## Don't copy

- **The ultramarine/near-black sections (37–63 s):** a dark world is a known rejection. Use the light-bowl idea only on a light, airy field.
- **Fades to black and silences between sections:** a compilation artefact. Our 66 s film needs continuous momentum.
- **No-type storytelling:** Apple can rely on product recognition. We still need the hook and the step words, legible and big, but placed in the world (reflected in the film, emerging from the island), never slapped on top.
- **Hands in hero shots:** the real hands here are photographed footage. If we use them, they must be photoreal (Higgsfield), never CG-uncanny.
- **The pink satin "architecture" taken literally:** our material is the soap-film bubble. Copy the camera grammar, not the metal.

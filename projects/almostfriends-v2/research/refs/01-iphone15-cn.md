**What it is.** A live-action product film about a pink iPhone 15. The camera is mounted on the phone or rides just above it for almost the whole film, while one person's day moves through home, staircase, rainy street, viewpoint, café, hotel, taxi, airport, cinema and aeroplane. Every feature gets one shot.

---

## 2. Why it reads high-end

The product is the one fixed thing in frame. The phone sits in the lower third at a low, raked angle, and the world swings, whips and changes around it, so every cut feels like motion instead of a slideshow. Each shot is lit like cinema: warm practical light, deep shallow-focus backgrounds (bokeh windows, plants, city), real reflections on the aluminium edge, and a gentle teal/amber grade. The UI on screen is never shown flat. It is always seen at a grazing perspective, close enough to fill the frame, so the Dynamic Island text (Flighty, Uber, calls) reads at headline size. Transitions are physical (whip pans with heavy directional blur, a hand or face passing the lens, the phone spinning), and they land on the song's accents. The film opens and closes on the same idea: the camera bump treated as a landscape. That bookend is what makes it feel authored.

## 4. Signature moves (特效)

### 4.1 Surface glide, with the object as landscape (0.5–2.5 s, bookended at 37.3–39 s)
- **Frame by frame:** at 0.5 the lens sits at the phone's surface level and the pink back fills the bottom 60 % like a desert floor. Over about 2 s the camera dollies forward and the camera bump rises from the horizon (0.5 → 2.0). Depth of field is extremely shallow: the bump goes from soft to sharp at about 1.5 s while the room behind is pure bokeh (plant, lemons). At 2.0 it reframes to a three-quarter view as it passes the lenses.
- **End:** the same grammar at 38.5. A low horizon, with the phone back as terrain and the bump as an architectural monument, under a full-frame cloudscape. The logo floats above the bump on the vertical centre line. Holds about 1.7 s.
- **How it's built:** probe/macro lens on a slider at the start. The end is CG (the phone back is rendered as a plane extending to the horizon, with an HDRI sky, atmospheric haze and a slow push).
- **For us:** raymarch the camera at the surface of Bub's liquid skin. Put the camera inside the SDF's tangent plane with a 14–18 mm-equivalent FOV at near-zero height and focus at 2–3 bubble radii. Render the skin as a horizon with its fresnel rim bright against a sky gradient, and use thick DOF (CoC from depth, 8–12 px max at 1080 wide).
- **Why it's cool:** a scale inversion. A hand-held object becomes a world. It reads as "cinematography" rather than "UI animation".

### 4.2 Phone-anchored rig, world swaps (6.0–9.0, 9–12, 20–33 s)
- The phone's top edge holds the same place and angle in frame across many shots: the screen's top bezel on a line about 62–65 % down the frame, tilted back about 60–70° from the lens, Dynamic Island centred. Only the background changes: room → stairwell → street → café → hotel → street → airport → red → plane.
- **Cut technique:** the cut happens under a foreground occluder (a hand at 7.3 s, a thumb at 21 and 27 s) or with the phone in the same position, so the eye stays locked on the device and the world "teleports".
- **How it's built:** a small camera rigged to the phone (snorricam-style), so the device stays locked while the operator walks. Backgrounds at f/1.4–2 bokeh.
- **For us:** this is the single biggest fix for the "1 Pick / 2 AI finds / 3 Chat / 4 It takes two" section. Lock our 3D iPhone at a grazing 60–70° tilt with its top edge at about 63 % frame height (in 1080×1920, that puts the screen top around y = 1200 and leaves the upper 60 % for the world). Swap the world behind it per beat (pastel liquid field → Bub's crowd → warm day-light "Day 1/2/3" world) with a hand-like occluder (a big soft bubble passing the lens) hiding each swap.

### 4.3 Whip pan and whip rotate with directional blur (12.0–12.2, 20.0–20.3, 27.6–28.3 s)
- 4–6 frames of extreme motion blur. At 20.2 s the phone, its photo and the table all smear diagonally at about 30° (see key frame). The whip *rotates* as well as pans: about 40–60° of roll at 20.0, near 180° at 27.6–28.3 (under the car, up the building).
- The incoming shot starts already moving and decelerates (ease-out over about 8–10 frames). It never starts from rest.
- **For us (WebGL):** a post-pass directional blur, sampling 16–24 taps along the velocity vector, with length tied to camera angular velocity. Peak blur about 8 % of frame width for 3 frames. Add camera roll of 30–90° across the whip. Curve: ease-in about 0.15 s into the whip, a cut or swap at peak blur, ease-out about 0.25 s. Put the swap at the frame with maximum blur so it is invisible.

### 4.4 Device fly-by past the lens (15.0–16.5 s)
- The phone rotates on its long axis from screen-facing to profile (15.0–15.3) and then swings across the lens so the pink back and camera bump fill 70 % of the frame (16.0). A face crosses in the foreground at 15.5 and 16.3 as a soft dark wipe. At 16.5 it lands in a different location (café).
- **For us:** our 3D phone should *move through* the camera's near plane at least once (near-clip fly-by with DOF blowing out the foreground edge), instead of always sitting framed and centred at 50 % scale.

### 4.5 Island-expand on a tap, at macro (21.0–23.0 s)
- Macro of a thumb resting on the island, with pad texture visible. At about 21.8 the thumb lifts and the black pill expands down and sideways into a large Flighty card (about 4.5 frames to settle, slight overshoot of a few px on the bottom edge). Green type at about 5 % of frame height, so it is fully legible.
- **For us:** this is the template for **match / SAME!!**. Instead of a sticker floating over a small phone, go to a grazing macro of the phone top, have Bub's pill (or the island) swell into the "SAME!!" card with a soft spring (stiffness about 260, damping about 22, 1 overshoot), and make the type 7–10 % of frame height.

### 4.6 Colour-light beat (31.4–33.0 s)
- Without changing composition, the scene floods red. The surroundings drop to near black; the screen glows pink-white; red bounces on the bezel. It is a one-shot mood shift on a musical accent.
- **For us:** on **SAME!!**, flood the background with coral (brand coral) for 0.5–0.8 s, then return. A lighting event, not just a graphic.

### 4.7 Push-through into the end world (34.5–38.0 s)
- The hand presses the phone to the aeroplane window. The phone tilts edge-on toward the clouds, and the camera follows it *through* the window frame. At 37.3 the phone back has become the ground plane. Then the logo.
- **For us:** the end mark: after "Friendship is the whole point." the camera dives into the merged blue+coral pair and comes out low on their surface, with the two bubbles as twin domes on a horizon under a soft pastel sky. "almost / friends.ai" sits above them on the centre line.

## 5. Type

- **Only one kind of on-screen type:** feature captions in Chinese white sans (PingFang-like regular, not bold). About 4 % of frame height (about 28 px on 720p), centred horizontally, placed in the negative space above the device, never on it: 灵动岛 at y ≈ 37 %, 防溅抗水 at y ≈ 49 %, 2倍长焦功能 left-of-centre at y ≈ 45 %, 4800万像素主摄 at y ≈ 49 %, 续航从早用到晚 at y ≈ 39 %.
- **Entry and exit:** a hard cut on with the shot or on a beat, and a hard cut off with the shot. No animation. Holds: 灵动岛 about 1.5 s (4.5–6.0), 防溅抗水 about 3 s (9.0–12.0), 2倍长焦功能 about 2 s (13.0–15.0), 4800万像素主摄 about 2 s (16.0–18.0), 续航 about 1 s (33.0–34.0), "iPhone 15" about 1.3 s (36.8–38.0).
- The product name "iPhone 15" (SF Pro, about 3.5 % height, white) sits exactly where the Apple logo replaces it. The logo is a direct swap, no transition.
- The bottom-right lyric subtitles are tiny (about 2 % height) and grey-white, a platform add-on, not design.
- **The real type in this film is the UI:** Flighty green numerals and "Go go go! / Vamos!" chat bubbles shot at macro, filling 40–60 % of frame width.

## 6. Colour, light, material

- **Grade:** warm amber mids, teal-green shadows, lifted blacks, soft highlight roll-off (filmic). Interiors are amber and walnut; exteriors are overcast teal-grey; the climax goes red and then dusky pink-gold clouds.
- **Key light:** large soft window light from the side (frames 0–8 s show windows as bokeh blocks). The phone's aluminium edge catches a thin bright rim (visible as a pink-gold line along the bezel in 6.0 s and 7.8 s). The screen is the brightest object in most frames.
- **Reflections:** the camera-bump glass shows a dark mirror with a sky reflection (38.5 s); the screen glass picks up light streaks (30.5 s) and a real water drop with refraction (11 s).
- **Depth:** everything except the device is out of focus. Background blur is roughly f/1.4 on a 35–50 mm lens at 20–30 cm.
- **Product colour:** dusty pink. It harmonises with the grade, so the product never fights the world.

## 7. Tempo & sound

- The track is "Wow" (Team Salut & DÜKI TRAN), an upbeat percussive pop/hip-hop loop. The RMS envelope (0.25 s windows) shows regular accents about every 1.0–1.75 s: 4.0, 6.0, 7.75, 9.75, 11.5, 13.0, 15.25, 16.75, 19.0, 20.75, 21.75, 23.25, 25.0, 27.25, 29.75, 31.5, 33.0, 35.25, 37.0.
- Cuts and whips land on or just before those accents. Two deliberate breaths: 3.0 s (−46 dB, before the phone lift) and 28–29 s (−31 to −33 dB, during the under-car roll).
- **Energy curve:** fast intro (0–4), steady feature parade (4–30), a lift into the red and plane section (31–37), a peak at 37.0, then a hard drop to silence for the logo (38–39). The silence under the logo is what makes the ending feel expensive.
- Natural sound is minimal; music carries the whole film.

## 8. Steal for almost friends

1. **Hook: open on a surface glide over Bub (4.1).** The current release opens at 0.0 s on a flat, frontal Bub face with "How to" type above. Instead, start frame 0 already moving: a macro camera skimming Bub's liquid skin, with its iridescent rim as the horizon and DOF bokeh bubbles beyond. Reveal the eyes as the camera rises over the curvature (about 1.2 s), then pull back to land "How to make more friends". This gives a moving frame 0 and an instant "cinema" read.
2. **Values pick to Chat (current 7–44 s): phone-anchored rig (4.2).** Currently the phone stands frontal, small (about 50 % frame height), dead centre on a white field with "1 Pick what matters to you" in small text at left. Instead: grazing 60–70° tilt, phone top edge locked at about 63 % frame height, screen filling the frame width, so the value chips (Family, Career, Adventure) read at headline size. Behind it, a deep-focus world (soft lit bubbles as bokeh blocks, warm window light), swapped per step under a passing foreground bubble.
3. **Values pick to Bub search: whip transitions (4.3).** Replace the current cross-fade into the pink bubble field (about 14.5–15 s) with a 0.4 s whip-rotate: about 45° roll, 16–24-tap directional blur peaking at 8 % frame width, swap at peak blur, incoming shot already moving and decelerating.
4. **Match / SAME!! (current 23–33 s): island-expand at macro (4.5) plus a colour-light beat (4.6).** Currently "SAME!!" is a blue sticker over a small phone with a few floating dots (32.0 s). Instead: macro on the phone top; a pill grows out of the island into the "SAME!!" card, with type at about 8–10 % frame height and a spring with one overshoot. On the same frame, flood the world coral for about 0.6 s, with coral bounce on the phone rim.
5. **Day 1/2/3: real light (6).** The current sun-glow blob behind the phone (34–39 s) is a flat radial gradient. Swap it for a moving key light: a window-light shadow pattern sweeping across the phone glass, with a light streak gliding across the screen (like 30.5 s), warmer each day (5200 K → 4200 K → 3400 K).
6. **Phone fly-by (4.4) at "It takes two yeses" to the unlock.** Have the phone swing past the lens (near-plane DOF blowout) and land in the unlock close-up, instead of the current blur-scale jump at 43.5 s.
7. **End mark: terrain bookend (4.1 and 4.7).** Currently the logo sits on a plain pale gradient (62–66 s). Instead, push through the merged pair onto their surface: two domes (blue + coral) on a low horizon under a soft pastel cloud sky (raymarched volumetric clouds or a Higgsfield plate). Put "almost / friends.ai" above, then cut the music to silence under the final mark for about 0.8 s.
8. **Type discipline (5).** One phrase per shot, placed in the negative space the camera composed for it, cut on with the shot and cut off with the shot. Never a label beside an object. Keep our type big (client rule), but adopt Apple's placement and hold logic: hold 1.5–3 s, a hard on/off on the beat.

## 9. Don't copy

- **The tiny captions (4 % height, regular weight).** These are exactly the "micro-label" look the client rejected. Copy where they sit and how long they hold, not their size.
- **Live-action handheld realism throughout.** Our film is code-rendered; faking 20 different real locations would look cheap. Borrow the *rig grammar* (locked device, moving world) and build worlds out of our own bubble materials. Use Higgsfield plates only for a sky or a single bokeh environment.
- **The flash frames at 27.6–27.76 s and the under-car roll.** Disorienting at our pace and in vertical format.
- **The dark, moody red tunnel as a whole section.** Use the colour-light *beat* briefly in brand coral; a long dark world is a known rejection.
- **The black legal card ending.**
